# The World Today — 20 November 2026

A newspaper front page drawn entirely in code (Pillow, `draw.py`), summing up Friday 20 November 2026 in one image. No AI-generated picture, no photos, no network — every pixel is drawn by the program.

Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 20 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120).

## What the image shows

- **Lead story — The Hague:** the International Court of Justice rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, 9–6 — the first such finding against a state.
- **Shenzhen summit:** Trump, Xi and Putin meet in the first three-way summit of the three leaders, issuing a joint declaration — but no breakthrough on Ukraine, Iran, or nuclear limits.
- **Kyiv:** Russian overnight strikes hit Kyiv's bridges, killing 8 and injuring 28; missiles and a drone strayed into Poland.
- **Markets:** Anthropic closed about 17% above its $83 IPO price on its Nasdaq debut, while the S&P 500 fell about 2% for a fifth straight session.
- **Riyadh:** a Houthi missile damages the Saudi sovereign wealth fund tower.
- **Washington:** the Senate passes the energy-permitting bill, 62–33.
- **Also today:** Kenya discharges its last Ebola patient; Cyclone Akvan makes landfall in Odisha; the EU pays Ukraine nearly €8bn; the IMF warns public debt is nearing 100% of world GDP; GTA 6 tops $2.3bn in sales.

## Reproduce

```sh
python3 draw.py
```

This writes `entry.png` (1024×1024), deterministically — running it twice produces identical bytes. (`python3 main.py` does the same.)
